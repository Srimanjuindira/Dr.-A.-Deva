About

I study how genes and environment together shape who is susceptible, or resistant, to disease. My doctoral work looked at the genetics and health of South Indian tribal populations, using markers such as blood group systems, autosomal DNA polymorphisms and the ACE gene.

From July 2019 to March 2025 I taught Zoology at Arignar Anna College (Arts and Science), Krishnagiri, where I also coordinated the IPR Cell. Before research, I worked as a clinical laboratory technician, so I am comfortable at the bench as well as in the classroom.

I am looking to work with a research team where I can apply and extend these skills.
